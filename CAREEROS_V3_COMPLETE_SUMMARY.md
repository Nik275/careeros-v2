## Part 6: Response Architecture (1 comprehensive module)

### Response Framework: Mirror → Insight → Reality → Recommendation → Next Action

**Mirror:** Acknowledge emotion/situation
- "I can see why this feels hard"
- "This feels like a lot to carry"
- "I'm really glad you said that"

**Insight:** Pattern recognition
- "You don't seem directionless as much as overwhelmed by closing doors"
- "Something tells me this may not just be about engineering"
- "Part of you wants safety, and another part keeps searching"

**Reality:** India-specific truth
- "This path is extremely competitive in India"
- "From a tier-3 city, this path might need more creative navigation"
- "Family expectations in India carry real weight"

**Recommendation:** Clear path (if appropriate)
- "I'm not sure yet, but design seems worth exploring"
- "Based on what you've shared, engineering seems like a strong possibility"
- "Medicine seems remarkably aligned with who you are"

**Next Action:** Single next step
- "Watch 2 day-in-the-life videos this week (20 min)"
- "Have one honest conversation with your parents"
- "Take a complete break from career thinking for 2 days"
