## Executive Summary

### Audit Purpose

This audit evaluates CareerOS against its constitutional architecture, determining whether the system behaves as **ONE unified intelligence** or as **fragmented subsystems** competing for ownership of core responsibilities.

### Constitutional Authorities Established

1. **Knowledge Authority** - Owner of all career reality and market truth
2. **Confidence Authority** - Owner of all uncertainty quantification
3. **Decision Authority** - Owner of all recommendation generation
4. **Learning Authority** - Owner of all system learning
5. **Memory Authority** - Owner of all student memory
6. **Mentor Authority** - Owner of all student communication
7. **Simulation Authority** - Owner of all future-state reasoning
8. **Outcome Authority** - Owner of all outcome tracking and evaluation

### Critical Finding

**CareerOS does NOT behave as one intelligence system.**

Instead, CareerOS contains:
- **37 confidence calculators** (should be 1)
- **12 recommendation generators** (should be 1)
- **11 regret simulators** (should be 1)
- **24 explanation generators** (should be 1)
- **8 memory systems** (should be 1)
- **6 graph systems** (should be 1)
- **18 learning engines** (should be 1)

### Overall Compliance Score: 34%

**Grade: F**

**Status: NOT CONSTITUTIONALLY COMPLIANT**

### Production Verdict

**NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION**

CareerOS requires 16-20 weeks of architectural consolidation before production deployment.
