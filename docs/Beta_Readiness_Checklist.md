## BETA CONSTRAINTS

### What We're Validating
- Product truth (does this actually help students?)
- Mentor quality (does it feel like a real mentor?)
- Safety (are vulnerable students protected?)
- Retention (do students naturally return?)
- India fit (does it work for Indian context?)

### What We're NOT Doing
- Scaling (30 students only)
- Feature requests (pattern-based only)
- Vanity metrics (focus on learning)
- Hype (quiet, focused beta)

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 70%+ say "helpful"
- [ ] 50%+ return naturally
- [ ] Time-to-wow <3 min
- [ ] 8+/10 mentor trust
- [ ] 0 unsafe incidents
- [ ] Voluntary recommendations
- [ ] Multiple "this gets me" moments

### Duration
- **Phase 1**: 2 weeks (onboarding validation)
- **Phase 2**: 4 weeks (retention validation)
- **Phase 3**: 4 weeks (deep engagement)
- **Total**: 10 weeks with 30 students

---

## GO/NO-GO DECISION

**RECOMMENDATION: GO**

CareerOS is ready for 30-student closed beta with the following conditions:

1. Clinical psychologist on-call for safety review
2. Daily safety monitoring for first 2 weeks
3. Weekly student interviews (5 students/week)
4. Immediate pause protocol if safety incident
5. No feature building during beta (learning only)

**Launch Date**: Ready immediately

**Risk Level**: LOW (with monitoring conditions)
